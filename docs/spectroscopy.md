# Spectroscopy and controls

Start with survey absorption, fluorescence excitation and emission on the same material used for chemical characterization. For Yb, scan the NIR region around the approximately 980 nm precedent and resolve crystal-field branches rather than imposing one fixed wavelength. Measure fluorescence decay with instrument response, temperature, optical power, concentration and fit model recorded. Absorption and fluorescence excitation can target the same transition when assignments and relaxation pathways are checked. Compare protiated and deuterated samples or dry and hydrated conditions as quenching probes. Track nearby O–H, N–H and C–H oscillators. [Y1–Y4](references.md#ytterbium-coordination-and-spectroscopy)

| Environment | What to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Solution | Fast speciation and survey spectra. Measure solvent and temperature shifts, exchange and broadening. |
| Dilute solid or cryogenic host | Identify host sites, strain, orientation, phonon broadening and spectral diffusion. Compare independent matched host preparations. |
| Gas or molecular beam | Verify intact transfer and composition before proposing high-resolution acquisition. Evaluate thermal decomposition and the viability of transferring ionic macrocycles. |

## Differential frequency protocol

1. Select a matched transition and isotope. Record line strength, measured FWHM, decay lifetime, centre uncertainty and drift as separate quantities. For Re and Ir, survey at the assigned optical transitions and quantify population decay, vibronic/solvent bandwidth and photodegradation. Consider a vibrational comparator such as the Re=O stretch only under its own signal model.
2. Calibrate the optical frequency against a traceable reference and record traceability, sampling, lock residuals and scan direction. Interleave coded A/B samples, use alternating or randomized blocks, exchange sample positions, and include same-sample comparisons, racemate or scaffold controls where interpretable. A racemate can mask a shift and can have distinct solid-state packing. Repeat with independent preparations and acquisition days.
3. Perturb temperature, pressure or solvent composition, electric and magnetic fields, power and polarization in controlled scans. Track isotope distribution, impurity, hydration, strain, instrument drift and blend asymmetry. Fit appropriate line models including overlapping branches, inspect residuals, quantify sensitivity to alternative line shapes and propagate correlated calibration and drift terms. Use blinded injected shifts to validate recovery before opening sample codes.
4. Define the primary estimator as the A-minus-B frequency centre with repeatability and systematic uncertainty. Sign reversal under relabelling alone is an algebraic check. Apparatus reversals, matched controls and independently reproduced batches test whether the effect follows the molecule. Prespecify the analysis and reporting threshold before unblinding.

The chat proposed $|\Delta\nu_{\rm PV}|/\Gamma_{\rm exp}$ as one screening metric, with $\Gamma_{\rm exp}$ the measured FWHM in Hz. Rank candidates using detection strength, collection efficiency, usable sample density and achievable differential uncertainty alongside that ratio. A line centre can be fitted more precisely than the FWHM, with precision set by signal, stability, acquisition time and the adequacy of the line model.
