# Physics and observables

The spin-independent electron–nucleus weak neutral current arises from virtual Z-boson exchange. Its molecular operator is odd under spatial inversion and even under time reversal. To first order, it gives opposite energy corrections to corresponding states localized in mirror-related molecular geometries. The proposal assumes chirality persists over preparation and interrogation. If tunnelling or interconversion matters, model populations and dynamics explicitly. [PV1, PV2](references.md#parity-violation-and-edm)

Use a fixed naming order A minus B for the full enantiomeric pair. Let $W_s(X)=\langle\Psi_s(X)|H_{\rm PV}|\Psi_s(X)\rangle$ for state $s$ of species $X$. At matched mirror geometries in equivalent environments, $W_s(B)=-W_s(A)$. The first-order ground-state enantiomer energy difference is $2W_g(A)$. The first-order differential transition frequency is

$$
\Delta\nu_{\rm PV}=\frac{[W_e(A)-W_g(A)]-[W_e(B)-W_g(B)]}{h}=\frac{2[W_e(A)-W_g(A)]}{h}.
$$

Use state expectations for the actual levels and include vibrational averaging where needed. For vibrational spectroscopy, the relevant difference can arise from two vibrational averages of the same electronic PV surface. A large ground-state correction alone leaves the transition signal undetermined. Ordinary circular dichroism, circularly polarized luminescence and field-dependent magneto-chiral dichroism characterize optical activity and can help with assignments. Their amplitudes do not determine this frequency difference. [PV1, PV2, Y2](references.md)

Atomic Yb experiments used interference between a weak-interaction-induced electric-dipole amplitude and a Stark-induced amplitude. The isotope-chain result supports the weak-charge mechanism and motivates isotope bookkeeping. It does not supply a molecular enantiomeric splitting. An electron EDM would give a P-odd and T-odd spin-dependent interaction. ThO and HfF⁺ experiments interrogate spin precession in a strong effective molecular electric field and use reversal channels to discriminate systematic shifts. This repository's proposed enantiomer comparison targets the P-odd, T-even weak interaction. [PV3–PV5](references.md#parity-violation-and-edm)
