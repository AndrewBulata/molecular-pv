# References

Identifiers are stable within this repository. Each entry describes the evidence used.

## Parity violation and EDM

- **PV1.** Laerdahl and Schwerdtfeger, *Phys. Rev. A* **60**, 4439 (1999). Four-component weak-interaction calculations for chiral molecules and a potential numerical benchmark. [Publisher](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.60.4439)
- **PV2.** *Toward Detection of the Molecular Parity Violation in Chiral Ru(acac)₃ and Os(acac)₃* (2022). Relativistic PV calculations and matrix-isolated infrared spectroscopy. The predicted PV response and recorded ordinary spectrum are separate outcomes. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9620138/)
- **PV3.** Antypas et al., *Nature Physics* **15**, 120–123 (2019). Measured atomic Yb isotope dependence. [Publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-018-0312-8)
- **PV4.** ACME Collaboration, *Nature* **562**, 355–360 (2018). ThO electron EDM limit from spin precession. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30333583/)
- **PV5.** Roussy et al., *Science* **381**, 46–50 (2023). HfF⁺ electron EDM search using trapped molecular ions. [Author-hosted paper](https://jila-pfc.colorado.edu/sites/default/files/2023-08/eEDM_Science_2023.pdf)

## Ytterbium coordination and spectroscopy

- **Y1.** BODIPY–bipyridine Yb(III) complexes, *Dalton Transactions* (2025), DOI 10.1039/D5DT00354G. Reports eight-coordinate examples and sensitized Yb emission around 980 nm. [Author manuscript at the publisher](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/getauthorversionpdf/d5dt00354g)
- **Y2.** *Helicene-Based Ligands Enable Strong Magneto-Chiral Dichroism in a Chiral Ytterbium Complex*. Chiral [Yb(hfac)₃(L)] and NIR magneto-chiral dichroism. [Publisher](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jacs.0c13180)
- **Y3.** *Equilibrium and kinetic studies on the formation of the lanthanide(III) complexes, [Ce(dota)]⁻ and [Yb(dota)]⁻* (1998). Condition-specific formation thermodynamics and kinetics. [Publisher](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/1998/dt/a705158a)
- **Y4.** *Structural and Chiroptical Properties of the Two Coordination Isomers of YbDOTA-Type Complexes*. SAP/TSAP assignment and NIR-CD of substituted derivatives. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2718833/)
- **Y5.** *Analysis of the Conformational Behavior and Stability of the SAP and TSAP Isomers of Lanthanide(III) NB-DOTA-Type Chelates*. Substitution, conformational preferences and stability constants. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3204396/)
- **Y6.** *A Bridge to Coordination Isomer Selection in Lanthanide(III) DOTA-tetraamide Complexes*. Bridged tetraamide and water-exclusion precedent. [Full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2556184/)

## Transition-metal comparators

- **T1.** *Chiroptical activity of benzannulated N-heterocyclic carbene rhenium(I) tricarbonyl halide complexes*. Enantiopure Re(I) examples and photophysical assignments. [Publisher](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlepdf/2024/cp/d3cp04300b)
- **T2.** *Chiral Iridium(III) Complexes in Light-Emitting Electrochemical Cells*. Isolated enantiomers, structures and photophysical comparisons. [Publisher](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsami.6b14050)
- **T3.** *A chiral rhenium complex with predicted high parity violation effects*. Synthesis and VCD of oxorhenium(VII) plus a predicted vibrational shift. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.08900)
