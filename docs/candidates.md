# Candidate designs and chemical validation

The chat reused **QV-YB-01** for several alternatives. It remains the Yb umbrella label. The identifiers below distinguish individual records. Formulae indicate intended coordination and charge. Where exact connectivity or stereochemistry is unresolved, freeze an atom-mapped design before producing coordinates or attempting synthesis.

| YB-HFAC | Neutral [Yb(hfac)₃(L)] with a 2,2′-bipyridine chelator, two stereogenic phenylethyl substituents and proposed 6,6′-methyl groups | Chat proposal. Compare R,R and S,S ligands and specify substitution sites and attachment connectivity. Three O,O hfac chelates plus N,N binding imply eight donors if retained. Test steric interference with binding and solvent displacement. |

| QV-YB-00 | [Yb(DOTA)]⁻, with axial solvent and counterion recorded separately | Literature complex and proposed project control. The N₄O₄ donor set permits SAP and TSAP forms and can have an additional axial donor. Unsubstituted DOTA has no fixed carbon stereocentre. Coordination conformers can be chiral and exchange. |
| YB-CDOTA | Yb(III) chelated by DOTA with a proposed single methyl substituent on a ring carbon | Chat proposal. Resolve site, atom mapping, absolute configuration and any CIP ambiguity before claiming a specific R/S structure. Retention of N₄O₄ binding and a dominant species are hypotheses. |
| YB-BRIDGE | Bridged or multiply substituted chiral DOTA-type macrocycle | Follow-on proposal. A Yb DOTA-tetraamide with a bridge and no inner-sphere water provides distinct literature precedent. Establish the candidate's own hydration, charge, stereochemistry and exchange. |

| RE-CT | Chiral Re(I) carbonyl chelate | Parallel proposal. Screen resolved chiral-at-metal or ligand-chiral Re(I) scaffolds and assign charge-transfer character. Re(VII) oxo vibrational PV is a separate literature comparison. |
| IR-CT | Δ/Λ cyclometalated Ir(III) | Parallel proposal. Screen resolved octahedral chelates. Compare mixed ligand-centred and charge-transfer states, with attention to oxygen sensitivity, vibronic broadening and photostability. |

Scaffold precedent comes from functionalized Yb(hfac)₃–bipyridines, chiral helicene adducts, Yb–DOTA derivatives, Re(I) carbonyls and enantiopure cyclometalated Ir complexes. The precise proposed substitutions and their performance remain open. [Y1–Y6, T1–T3](references.md)

## Chemical feasibility workflow

1. Record synthetic precedent at the exact ligand or closest scaffold level. For YB-HFAC, assess ligand assembly from configurationally defined building blocks and substitution at a solvated Yb(hfac)₃ precursor. Compare a less sterically crowded bipyridine if 6,6′ substitution impedes chelation. For YB-CDOTA, trace a route from a configurationally defined C-substituted cyclen derivative through protected pendant-arm installation, deprotection and metalation. Assess regioisomer separation, retention of configuration and metalation speciation. For Re and Ir, compare chiral-ligand assembly with resolution of the finished coordination complex, then test configurational stability. These are route assessments with conditions to be selected from the relevant precedent.
2. Establish composition with orthogonal methods such as HRMS with isotope-pattern assignment, elemental analysis or metal assay, and chromatography. Use single-crystal XRD for donor geometry where possible. Record absolute-structure evidence and uncertainty separately. Treat a crystal as a sample of the solid phase and check solution species independently.
3. Measure ligand and complex enantiomeric purity with a validated chiral separation where feasible. Use ECD, VCD or NIR-CD for complementary assignment. Link sign to configuration by an independently assigned reference, diffraction, or validated chiroptical modelling. Mirror-image ligand certificates alone leave metal-centred helicity and diastereomer populations unresolved.
4. Match the full pairs, including any coordination helicity, relative ligand configurations, hydration, counterion, isotope distribution and conformer populations. Use variable-temperature or exchange NMR where paramagnetic resolution permits, time-resolved optical spectra, and orthogonal chromatography. Test ligand loss, axial solvent exchange and coordination-isomer exchange separately over the acquisition window. Track free ligand and free metal with suitable standards and assess dilution, solvent, pH, competing ligands and sample age.
5. Advance pairs with reproducible composition and mirror-related ensembles across independent preparations. Quantify any residual diastereomer or hydration imbalance and its expected line-centre bias. A formation constant is an equilibrium quantity. Establish kinetic stability in the actual measurement environment. [Y3–Y6](references.md#ytterbium-coordination-and-spectroscopy)
